Ferretería Rueda operated in Barranquilla without a digital sales channel. The goal was to build its first e-commerce store from scratch, aligned with the physical catalog and fully manageable by the team without code changes.

## Custom Shopify theme

I developed a custom theme with Liquid and JavaScript on Shopify: reusable sections, snippets, and blocks, combined with interactive components to improve navigation and usability without breaking the platform's native compatibility.

## What I implemented

- Complete shopping experience: predictive search, dynamic filters, and a persistent cart.
- Interactive components to improve catalog navigation and usability.
- Technical SEO on Shopify, with organic indexing and ranking of products and categories on Google.
- Reusable, fully configurable section architecture managed from the admin, with no code changes.
- Hierarchical category menus and automatic discount badges on sale products.

<Callout>
  The result is the company's first digital sales channel: an online catalog that reflects the physical inventory, with a clear shopping experience and content the team can rearrange without technical intervention.
</Callout>
